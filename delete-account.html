Delete Account & Data

If you no longer wish to use Noxie, you can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data. We provide easy ways to request this deletion directly from the mobile application or by contacting our support team.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your game progress, streaks, lightnings, league rankings, and profile data cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account (In-App)

The fastest and easiest way to delete your data is directly through the Noxie application:

  1. Open the Noxie app on your device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile screen.
  3. Tap the Settings icon (the gear in the top corner).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the warning prompt and confirm your choice.

Upon confirmation, your personal data and account details will be immediately scheduled for permanent erasure from our servers.

Important Notice About Subscriptions

If you have an active Legend Premium Subscription, securely managed through the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android), deleting your Noxie account does not cancel your subscription billings.

You must manually cancel your subscription through your device's settings to avoid future charges:

  • iOS (Apple): Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > Noxie > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android (Google Play): Open Google Play > Tap Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Noxie > Cancel subscription.

Already Uninstalled the App?

For security and verification purposes, account deletion can only be initiated from within the authenticated Noxie application. If you have already uninstalled the app and wish to delete your account and data, please reinstall Noxie from the App Store or Google Play Store, sign in to your account, and follow the in-app deletion steps above.
